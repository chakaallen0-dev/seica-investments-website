About Seica Investments

Waterproofing solutions for every structure.

Seica Investments is a Botswana-based company specializing in roof waterproofing and leak repairs for residential and commercial properties.

We assess each project's needs and recommend suitable waterproofing systems, from hydrophobic sealants and bitumen coatings to torch-on membranes, liquid rubber and cementitious solutions.

  • Solutions tailored to roof and surface conditions
  • Quality-focused application and workmanship
  • Clear, system-specific guarantee periods

8–15 year waterproofing guarantees.

Guarantee periods depend on the waterproofing system installed and the applicable written warranty terms.

  • Hydrophobic sealant — 8 years
  • Bitumen — 10 years
  • Cementitious — 15 years
  • Torch-on membrane — 15 years
  • Liquid rubber — 15 years

Roof painting: A New Look Promise focused on appearance, not a waterproofing or long-term performance guarantee.
